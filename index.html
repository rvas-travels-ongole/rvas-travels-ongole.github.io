Good to know

      

        
How is the fare decided?
The fare depends on the car, the kilometres and the number of days. We tell you the full amount on the confirmation call, before your trip is booked.

        
Are tolls and parking included?
Tolls and parking are paid separately unless your quote says they are included. Ask us on the call and we will tell you exactly.

        
Where do you pick up from?
We pick up from Ongole and towns within about 50 km, such as Chirala, Kandukur, Addanki, Singarayakonda, Chimakurthi and Podili. Your drop can be anywhere in India. For a pickup farther away, call us and we will check.

        
Can you go outside Andhra Pradesh?
Yes. Our cars carry an All India tourist permit, so we can take you to any state.

        
How do reward points work?
You earn 1 point for every kilometre you travel with us. 100 points give ₹10 off a later trip. Tick “Use my saved points” when you book, or ask us for your balance on WhatsApp.

        
How do I change or cancel a trip?
Open My trips and tap Cancel or Change on the trip, or call us any time. The request goes to us on WhatsApp.
